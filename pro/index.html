Questions

Everything you might ask.

Is Detail Connect really free to start?+

Yes. You can build your booking page and take bookings on the free Starter plan. Upgrade to Pro in the app when you want add-ons, upsells, and revenue analytics.

Do my clients need to download anything?+

No. Clients just tap your booking link in any browser, pick a time, and book. You run everything from the app; they don't need an account.

How do payments work?+

Send professional invoices the moment you finish a job, and track tips and earnings in the app. In-app payment processing is expanding — see the app for what's live in your area.

What kind of support is there?+

Real people, 24/7, with a guaranteed response within 48 hours. Detail Connect is veteran-owned and built by detailers who use it themselves.
